• One entry point for mainstream models: OpenAI GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Qwen, OpenRouter

【Describe it — the app builds it】
Stop hunting through menus. Say what you want and OneBox turns it into a working tool or app:
• "Give me 8 candidates for what to eat today." — AI create on the decision wheel fills in the options for you
• "Travel planner." "Article style converter." — AI builds the whole app, screens and tools included; refine it by chatting: "change the button to blue, add a date picker"
• Answers arrive as real controls — forms, sliders, date, time, color and file pickers rendered natively in the chat, not a wall of text
• Generate images from a text prompt, then edit, compress or convert them with the same toolbox
• Every tool that needs input has an AI create entry: describe it, and the AI writes it for you

【DSH client: your computer's AI agent, from your phone】
Running DeepSeek Harness (DSH)? Scan a QR code to pair, then approve, follow up or interrupt from anywhere, over LAN or an end-to-end encrypted relay. Works with the open-source onebox-dsh-bridge plugin.
